# Aurelia Bath — Landing page brief

Built with the `landing-page-design` skill. Aurelia Bath is a fictional prototype brand, so names, prices and figures below are believable samples. Replace every one of them with real data before launch.

## Assumptions

| Question | Assumption |
|---|---|
| One primary action | Book a free bathroom design consultation |
| Offer | 45 minute consultation at the Bangsar showroom or on video, followed by a layout, matched product list, rough in drawings and one itemised quote within 3 working days |

## 2. Hero copy

- **Headline:** Your whole bathroom, planned as one suite
- **Subheadline:** Basins, mixers, baths, WCs and showers designed to match. We plan every piece around your floor plan and give your contractor the fitting drawings, so it all goes in right the first time.
- **CTA:** Book a free consultation
- **Proof:** Rated 4.8 by 517 homeowners

## 3. Benefits

- **One finish across the room** — mixers, shower set, rails and drain covers come from the same production batch, so every brass piece matches.
- **Fitted right the first time** — your contractor gets rough in drawings with exact pipe heights before tiling. 97.4% of installs need no return visit.
- **One itemised quote** — every fixture, delivery and the handover check on a single quote. Most final invoices land within 6% of the first estimate.
- **Delivered in 19 working days** — stocked pieces reach Klang Valley and Singapore sites in one delivery. Made to order finishes take about 7 weeks.
- **Guaranteed for 15 years** — 15 years on ceramics and solid surface, 7 years on brassware, serviced by our own technicians.

## 4. How it works

1. **Book a 45 minute consultation** at the Bangsar showroom or on video. Bring a floor plan, photos or a rough idea.
2. **Get your plan in 3 working days**: layout, matched product list, rough in drawings and one itemised quote. Yours to keep, even if you buy elsewhere.
3. **We deliver and check the fit**: one delivery to site, and our technician checks every connection with your contractor at handover.

## 5. FAQ

1. How much does a full bathroom cost? Most main bathrooms land between RM 14,800 and RM 31,500 for fixtures, before tiling and labour. The consultation ends with an itemised quote.
2. Is the consultation really free? Yes. The session, layout and product list cost nothing, with no obligation to order.
3. Can my own contractor install the pieces? Yes. We send rough in drawings before tiling and join the handover to check every connection.
4. How long does delivery take? 19 working days for stocked pieces in the Klang Valley and Singapore. About 7 weeks for made to order finishes.
5. What does the warranty cover? 15 years on ceramics and solid surface, 7 years on brassware and cartridges, 2 years on Senso WC electronics.
6. Can I see the finishes before I decide? Visit the Bangsar showroom or ask for the free finish sample box.
7. Do you work with interior designers and architects? Yes. Specification sheets, CAD and BIM files, and a dedicated trade contact.
8. Do you deliver outside Malaysia and Singapore? Brunei too. Other countries on request.
9. What if I change my mind after ordering? Unused stocked pieces can be returned within 30 days. Made to order pieces can be changed until production starts.

## 6. SEO and AEO

**Index.** The consultation is an evergreen offer and matches search intent such as "bathroom renovation Kuala Lumpur" and "matching bathroom fittings".

- **Title:** Aurelia Bath | Matching bathroom suites, planned and fitted
- **Meta description:** Basins, mixers, baths, WCs and showers designed to match. Book a free 45 minute consultation and get a fitted plan and itemised quote in 3 working days.
- FAQ is rendered as plain question and answer HTML plus `FAQPage` JSON-LD.

## 7. Layout

**A. Classic hero plus sections.** The product is physical and visual, so a hero photo of a finished room explains the offer in one look. The argument is short enough that a long form story (B) is not needed, and the traffic is not high intent enough for a minimal page (C).
